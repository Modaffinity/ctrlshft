# Philosophy and Humanities Domain

Use for philosophy, history of ideas, humanities, interpretation, and conceptual genealogy.

| Priority | Sources | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, primary texts | Prefer durable canonical sources. |
| 2 | PhilPapers, university-hosted bibliographies | Access may fail; record gaps plainly. |
| 3 | Google Books, publisher pages, journal abstracts | Useful for triangulation, not enough alone for interpretation. |

Recency is usually not a quality signal. Do not prefer recent sources unless the question is explicitly about current scholarship.

When sources disagree, present the competing interpretations and what would change the assessment.
