# Business Domain

Use for market, company, industry, financing, legal entity, and competitive research.

| Priority | Sources | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEC EDGAR, Companies House, company investor relations | Prefer filings and primary disclosures over summaries. |
| 2 | Patents, regulator records, court dockets | Use for product claims, defensibility, and risk. |
| 3 | Reputable trade press | Good for leads; verify load-bearing claims elsewhere. |

Recency half-life: months. Old market facts decay quickly.

Known limits: Crunchbase and OpenCorporates have no usable free tier for this skill path. If a paid database would materially change the answer, state the gap rather than padding.
