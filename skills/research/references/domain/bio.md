# Biology and Life Science Domain

Use for biomedical, clinical, life-science, and biology-adjacent research.

| Priority | Sources | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PubMed/NCBI, ClinicalTrials.gov, regulator labels | Prefer primary biomedical indexes and official trial records. |
| 2 | Europe PMC, bioRxiv, journal pages | Separate preprints from peer-reviewed evidence. |
| 3 | Reviews and guidelines | Useful synthesis; verify key claims against cited primary work. |

Recency half-life: about 18 months, shorter for fast-moving clinical topics.

For NCBI E-utilities, `tool=` and `email=` must be registered with NCBI before relying on them as identity.

This reference does not make medical recommendations. For user-facing medical advice, stop and route to appropriate safety guidance.
